NeuroViu Disability-Inclusive Clinical Research Accessibility Toolkit (v1.0)

A cross-disability research toolkit providing accessibility planning checklists, participant accommodation forms, consent and communication guidance, participant autonomy procedures, and research improvement resources.

Available in PDF and editable Word formats.

Developed by NeuroViu. Accessibility verification and ongoing improvements are documented as part of the toolkit's maintenance process.
