# NeuroLearn — Student Activity Sheet

**Free classroom activity · 20–30 minutes · No hardware required**

Open https://neuroviu.github.io/openbrain/#lessons

## Learning objectives
- Explain what EEG, EOG, and EMG measure at a high level.
- Identify the approximate frequency ranges of five EEG rhythm bands.
- Distinguish simulated signals from measured physiological data.

## Activity 1: Observe
1. Open the Biosignal Explorer and select EEG, ViuGaze™, and EMG in turn.
2. Write one difference you observe between their simulated waveforms.
3. Explain why the signals cannot be used to infer a person's diagnosis.

## Activity 2: Compare rhythms
1. Open the Brain Rhythm Explorer.
2. Compare 2 Hz delta, 10 Hz alpha, and 20 Hz beta examples.
3. Over a 2-second interval, estimate how many cycles each completes.
4. Download a CSV and identify the time and amplitude columns.

## Activity 3: Check understanding
Complete all four NeuroLearn lessons and answer each quiz correctly. Record one new concept and one question you still have.

## Reflection
- What is the difference between a synthetic signal and a real recording?
- Why do artifacts and recording context matter?
- How could open-source software help neuroscience education?

**Safety:** All OpenBrain waveforms are mathematical demonstrations, not recordings from humans or validated clinical models. Do not use this activity for diagnosis or treatment.
