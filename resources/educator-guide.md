# NeuroLearn — Educator Guide

**Suggested level:** Secondary school through introductory undergraduate. **Time:** 20–30 minutes.

## Preparation
Students need a modern web browser. No login, headset, sensors, or uploads. Visit https://neuroviu.github.io/openbrain/.

## Facilitation
1. Introduce EEG (scalp voltage differences), EOG (eye-movement-associated electrical changes), and EMG (muscle activation-associated electrical activity).
2. Let students explore the synthetic waveform controls and frequency bands.
3. Ask learners to complete the four short lessons and quiz questions.
4. Discuss why signal frequency by itself cannot reveal a person's thoughts, emotions, or diagnosis.

## Answer guidance
- In a 2-second interval, 2 Hz ≈ 4 cycles, 10 Hz ≈ 20 cycles, 20 Hz ≈ 40 cycles.
- Synthetic waveforms are generated by equations; real recordings contain multiple sources, artifacts, and measurement constraints.
- The CSV amplitude field uses arbitrary units, not calibrated microvolts.
- Quiz correct choices by lesson: EEG = B; rhythms = C; EOG = A; EMG = B.

## Accessibility and differentiation
Use keyboard-accessible form controls, allow extra time, provide the text explanations instead of relying solely on chart interpretation, and offer a written response alternative. Check the experience with the assistive technologies used by your class; independent accessibility certification has not been performed.

## Assessment
Use the student activity sheet and reflection prompts for formative assessment. The browser-generated completion certificate is **informal recognition of four quiz answers**, not accreditation, professional training, or proof of identity.
