Brain Rhythm Explorer

Compare five commonly described EEG frequency bands. These are illustrative oscillations—not actual brain recordings. Frequency bands overlap across studies, and a frequency alone cannot tell us a person's mental state.

Waveform: pure synthetic sine wave; 2-second display window; amplitude in arbitrary units. No EEG hardware or personal data.

What are we exploring?

🧠 EEG

Electroencephalography measures electrical activity recorded from the scalp. Explore an illustrative rhythmic waveform.

👁️ ViuGaze™

Our name for exploring eye-movement and blink signals based on electrooculography (EOG).

💪 EMG

Electromyography measures electrical activity associated with muscle activation.
